# RouteMuse — Judge Q&A

## Why would anyone pay £5.99?

The core planner is free, so users can make a useful first decision without a paywall. The £5.99 RouteMuse Pass is for the moment a plan becomes a real trip: the group needs confidence, coordination and a trip-ready view. It is a one-off payment, not a subscription they forget to cancel.

## Why not just use Google Maps or a generic AI itinerary generator?

Maps is useful for navigation and generic AI can produce ideas. RouteMuse is designed for the specific planning annoyance before a trip: bringing a group’s options and trade-offs into one simple plan so a decision is easier to make and share. The pitch must only describe capabilities the live build demonstrates; we do not claim that RouteMuse replaces a navigation app or books travel itself.

## How do affiliate links avoid bias?

RouteMuse uses **outbound booking referrals**, not pay-to-rank listings. Recommendation order never changes because of affiliate commission. Users can still see non-affiliated options, and an eligible referral is clearly labelled before the user leaves RouteMuse. **No ads. No pay-to-rank. No hidden price uplift.**

## What is the first metric you would measure after launch?

**Activated-plan completion rate:** the percentage of visitors who create and share a simple trip plan. It tests whether RouteMuse solves the core annoyance before the team optimises monetisation. The next measures are Pass conversion and completed outbound referral bookings.

## Who pays, in one sentence?

**Users pay for confidence when plans change; partners pay for completed intent.**
