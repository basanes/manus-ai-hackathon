# RouteMuse Monetisation Pitch

## 30-second judge explanation

> RouteMuse is free to start because travel planning is episodic and users should see real value before they pay. The Free Planner lets a group create one simple trip and share it. When coordination and disruption risk become real, they can buy a **£5.99 RouteMuse Pass per trip**—one payment, no subscription—for the trip-ready experience. We also use clearly labelled **outbound booking referrals**: partners pay only for completed intent, never for ranking. **Users pay for confidence when plans change; partners pay for completed intent.** No ads, no pay-to-rank and no hidden price uplift.

## One simple unit-economics hypothesis

**Assumption, not live performance or a forecast:** for every 1,000 active planners in a measurement period, if 5% buy the £5.99 Pass, gross Pass revenue is **£299.50** before tax, refunds, payment fees and product-serving costs.

```text
1,000 active planners × 5% Pass conversion × £5.99 = £299.50 gross Pass revenue
```

Affiliate revenue is deliberately excluded from this first hypothesis until RouteMuse has actual partner terms and completed-booking data. When available, the formula is:

```text
completed outbound referral bookings × agreed commission rate (2–8% where available)
```

## What this does—and does not—claim

- **Current model:** £0 Free Planner; £5.99 RouteMuse Pass per trip; clearly labelled outbound booking referrals.
- **Later-only option:** £19/year for frequent travellers; concierge/community white-label workspace. Neither is an MVP promise.
- **Not claimed:** current revenue, signed partners, active commission contracts, live booking conversion or a live booking service.
- **Trust rule:** recommendation order never changes because of affiliate commission.
